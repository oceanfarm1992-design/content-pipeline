- **Species / age:** human, 28
- **Appearance:** Mia has a slender build, olive skin, and a heart-shaped face with high cheekbones. Her expressive almond-shaped hazel eyes are framed by long, dark lashes. She has shoulder-length wavy chestnut hair that she often wears down, reflecting her casual style.
- **Clothing:** Mia wears a fitted navy blue t-shirt, light denim jeans, and a pair of white sneakers. She accessorizes with a simple silver necklace and a slim wristwatch.
- **Never changes:** hazel eyes,  chestnut hair,  silver necklace
- **Personality:** empathetic, resilient, determined,  impulsive, struggles with trusting others
- **Strengths / weaknesses:** intuitive, emotionally intelligent, resourceful / can be overly emotional, has difficulty accepting help

Wound: abandoned by her father for her fascination with technology over emotional connection.. Fear: losing her autonomy and being manipulated by technology.. Arc: Mia's journey is one of reclaiming her agency and realizing the power of her emotional connections..
