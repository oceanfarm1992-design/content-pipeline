# Channel brief

The showrunner reads this before developing each new series. Edit it to steer the channel; the next monthly
run picks it up. To fix a specific concept for a series, add its row to `seasons_plan.csv` (season number, and
at least a logline; leave title, arc or ending empty for the showrunner to develop).

Series 1 (Nov 2026): "A good AI robot tries to save a human from a bad AI robot."

## Format

- Standalone vertical (9:16) micro-drama series, 30 episodes of 60 seconds, one episode a day for a month.
- Each episode ends on a hook that makes the viewer need the next one; the series ends on a satisfying payoff.
- After the last episode is posted, all 30 are joined into one ~30-minute full movie and uploaded once, so the
  series must also play well back to back (no recap padding; each episode's hook flows into the next).
- The series title states the whole premise in one line, like:
  "My Dog Fought the World for Me", "Come Home a Billionaire After Betrayal", "She Dumped Me Then Begged Me Back".

## What works (from TikTok Series catalogs, Nov 2026)

- **Reversal fantasy:** an underdog is betrayed, humiliated or thrown out → a hidden identity, power or ally
  surfaces → comeback, and the people who wronged them regret it. The biggest hits are pure reversal
  ("Global Assets Shrank by a Factor of 10 Million" 13.5M views, "My Daughter Calls Him Daddy" 1.4M).
- **A loyal non-human ally is a proven hook:** an animal ("My Dog Fought the World for Me" 3.4M, "The Intern Who
  Speaks to African Beasts" 60K) or a machine (a protective robot or AI). Real animals don't talk on screen (the
  lead may hear their thoughts as voice-over); robots can speak, since they have no lips to sync.
- Strong, clear emotions: betrayal, loyalty, family secrets, revenge, protective love, romance.

## Hard limits of our production

- Talking faces have no lip-sync yet: dialogue is heard as voice while the shot shows reactions, hands,
  profiles, backs, over-the-shoulder, or the listener. No long front-on close-ups of a speaking mouth.
- At most 3 characters in a shot; one clear action per 5-second shot; simple camera moves.
- 3-6 recurring locations per series; leads keep the same face, hair and clothing all series.
- Keep it suitable for a general audience: no gore, no sexual content.
